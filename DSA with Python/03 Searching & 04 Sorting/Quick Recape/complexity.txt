sorting             ||  Time Complexity                                         ||  Space Complexity
1. Bubble Sort      ||  Best: O(n) Avg: O(n^2) Worst: O(n^2)                    || O(1)
2. Selection Sort   ||  Best: O(n^2), Avg: O(n^2), Worst: O(n^2)                || O(1)
3. Insertion Sort   ||  Best: O(n), Avg: O(n^2), Worst: O(n^2)                  || O(1)
4. Merge Sort       ||  Best: O(n log n), Avg: O(n log n), Worst: O(n log n)    || O(n)


Remember these shortcuts
- Bubble Sort: Optimized version gets O(n) best case.
- Selection Sort: Always O(n²) comparisons.
- Insertion Sort: Very efficient for already-sorted or nearly sorted data.
- Merge Sort: Consistently O(n log n), but requires extra space.
